Built for the set

The things that matter when the camera's rolling: clean light, control you can trust, and a rig that tells you the truth about its state.

🎥

Flicker-free on camera

High-frequency PWM with 16-bit, dithered dimming — no banding on camera, and fades that stay smooth with no visible stepping.

🛡️

Protection in microseconds

Per-port over-current is caught in microseconds — faster than a fuse or a PSU surge — with short and thermal foldback that rides through a fault instead of just tripping.

🎛️

DMX, CRMX & RDM

Wired DMX or CRMX wireless (LumenRadio), with full RDM — discovery, remote addressing and sensor feedback from the desk.

🎬

21 film-ready effects

Fire, candle, lightning, TV glow, passing headlights, faulty fluorescent, paparazzi and more — with rig-wide tempo and spread.

🎨

Per-port colour & curves

RGB or tuneable white on every output, with a Hue/Sat/Kelvin picker, dimmer curves and per-channel trim.

📈

Live telemetry

Per-port current — calibrated to within 1% of a bench meter — plus temperature, faults and source, so the rig tells you the truth about its state.

📶

Runs from your phone

Set up and run everything over Bluetooth. The control path never touches a network: no cloud, no account, no tracking.

⬆️

Wireless updates

Firmware straight over Bluetooth, with automatic rollback if a build ever misbehaves.

Your rig, in your pocket

The companion app talks straight to the controller over Bluetooth — set DMX addresses, mix colour, fire effects, save scenes and watch live telemetry. It collects nothing and needs no sign-in.
